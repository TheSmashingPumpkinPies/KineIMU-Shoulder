## Result

**CP1 passed at the synthetic numerical and evidence-interface scope.**
`kineimu_shoulder.shoulder.long_axis_elevation` accepts an M2.4
`RelativeOrientationResult`, both explicit alignment records, side, source
type and a positive caller-declared sample gap. It rotates H +Z by valid
`q_TH` and computes `atan2(hypot(u_x,u_y),u_z)` in radians. The alignment
transforms were applied upstream; CP1 checks their identity and status and
does not compose them twice. Each output retains the M2 grid and artifact,
source hashes, alignment records, validity, exact reason and evidence label.

The analytical E0–E4 cases cover neutral, right flexion, right/left
abduction and 180° reversal. E5 uses noncommuting thorax/humerus rotations
and the independent `acos(sqrt(2)/4)` expectation. E6 verifies quaternion
sign invariance. Tests also reject absent, mismatched, expired and remounted
alignment; absent or invalid clock/heading evidence; nonfinite/nonunit valid
quaternions; and preserve an M2 invalid reason. Assumed evidence remains
`Assumed/Experimental`. Numerical validity and physical anatomical evidence
eligibility are separate. Synthetic and assumed cases cannot claim the latter.
