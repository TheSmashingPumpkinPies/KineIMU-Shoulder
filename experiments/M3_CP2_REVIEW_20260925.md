# M3.2 CP2 Review — Relative Angular Speed

Date: 2026-09-25 (Asia/Shanghai). Source contract:
`protocols/M3_KINEMATICS_CONTRACT.md`; independent expected values:
`tests/fixtures/M3_KNOWN_MOTION.md`.

## Result

**CP2 passed at the synthetic numerical and evidence-interface scope.**
`kineimu_shoulder.shoulder.relative_angular_speed` consumes an M2.4
`RelativeOrientationResult`, two explicit alignment records, side, source
type and a positive caller-declared `max_sample_gap_us`. It reuses CP1's
row/evidence gate, then computes the principal angle of
`inverse(q_TH[i]) ⊗ q_TH[i+1]` divided by the observed positive common-time
delta for each adjacent valid pair. The output has separate start/end
timestamps, speed in rad/s, validity and exact reason per interval. Its
nested CP1 result retains original M2 clock/heading artifacts, alignment
records, source hashes and row reasons. It never bridges an invalid row.

S0–S3 test zero motion, 30° over 0.25 s, 20°/50° over irregular 0.2/0.5 s,
and quaternion sign reversal against independent analytical values. S4
rejects a gap one microsecond over the declared maximum and admits equality.
S5 preserves `m2_invalid:interpolation_gap` for both adjacent intervals;
endpoint invalidity takes precedence over a simultaneous gap. S6 records
the unavoidable endpoint alias: a known 360° physical path with identity
endpoints produces zero principal endpoint speed, which cannot establish
physical stationarity. A noncommuting X-to-Y rotation yields a positive
3-D speed even while both long-axis elevations equal 90°, proving this
quantity is not signed elevation derivative. Nonunit endpoints and missing
global evidence yield NaN speed with stable reasons. Assumptions remain
`Assumed/Experimental`; synthetic data is never anatomical validation.
