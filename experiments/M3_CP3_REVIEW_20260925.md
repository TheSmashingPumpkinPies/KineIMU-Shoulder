# M3.3 CP3 Review — Declared Interval ROM and Duration

## Result

**CP3 passed at the synthetic numerical and evidence-interface scope.**
`kineimu_shoulder.shoulder.interval_rom_duration` takes an M2.4 relative
orientation stream, two alignment records, a declared side/source type,
positive gap limit, and exact common-time start/end microseconds. It reuses
CP1's evidence and elevation checks, then calculates elevation maximum minus
minimum in radians and actual elapsed endpoint time in seconds over the
inclusive interval. The returned CP1 result retains original M2 clock,
heading, alignment, source hashes, row validity and reasons.

R0–R2 recover analytical 70°, 55° and 50° ranges and observed 1.2, 0.9
and 0.5 s durations from triangular/asymmetric and subinterval traces. R3
rejects a nonmember endpoint without interpolation; R4/R5 reject reversed
and equal bounds. R6 excludes the whole interval for an invalid interior
row, retaining its index and exact `m2_invalid:interpolation_gap` reason.
R7 excludes a gap one microsecond above the declared maximum, while equality
passes. Additional tests cover out-of-range bounds, a single row, multiple
invalid reasons, row-versus-gap precedence, irrelevant invalid rows outside
the request, missing alignment and assumed evidence. Excluded intervals have
NaN for both numerical outputs and an explicit reason.
