## Compatibility and analytical decisions

- M2 order remains sensor calibration → R_NS → node AHRS → R_NK once;
  specific force is +g at rest, angular rate is body-frame derivative and
  initialization is explicit synthetic truth. Default B sensor force is +X;
  flexion gyro is -Z, left-abduction gyro +Y. Matrix/vector arithmetic checked
  these independently without importing any project numerical function.
- Rates at piecewise-linear knots use left derivatives matching interval-ending
  observations; noncommuting smooth portions retain analytic right-endpoint
  discretization and require an independent100/200Hz convergence check.
- M4 20°/10° confirmed envelopes yield80.1° ROM and3.94s duration for
  the physical90° /4.5s support. Plateau maxima form a set for S/Q peak-time
  error; E still asserts earliest exact maximum, and S/Q report its offset.
  This avoids a numerically insignificant plateau drift creating a false
  one-second peak-time error; no production M4 definition is changed.
- Clean1° and W3° downstream angle targets are engineering budgets, with
  per-node sub-budgets and doubled ROM/range/SD propagation. They are not
  derived by converting M2's Cartesian tolerance into claimed sensor accuracy.
  Timing budgets follow sampling resolution and35°/s slowest ordinary slope;
  speed/hold have their own gates and will be measured separately.
- Long low-bias/end-ramp levels are .002°/s/.004°/s: an ideal300s integrated
  error budget is .6°. High long levels are explicitly stress, not accidentally
  labelled working domain. Clock scale, sensor bias and world-yaw isolation are
  distinct mechanisms; yaw common-mode invariance does not validate proxy drift.
- The jitter×loss W interaction uses periodic single-sample loss, preserving
  the50ms gap limit. One four-sample-packet deletion alone gives50ms equality;
  actual jitter combined with that packet deletion could exceed the bound and
  must not be silently treated as a guaranteed W input.
- Finite seed sessions are the repeated units; frame/rep pseudo-replication,
  hidden seed selection and valid-survivor-only error summaries are prohibited.
  Pressure with unobservable bias/false maps has documented limits, while
  observable bad time/gaps/missing evidence have explicit required rejections.
- Raw/schema/firmware/hardware/uv.lock and accepted M2/M3/M4 contracts are
  unchanged. Hardware remains frozen. No physical anatomical, human or clinical
  accuracy claim and no new dependency is made.
